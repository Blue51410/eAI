# 🔒 Privacy Disclaimer

eAI does not collect any data. eAI Lite has no accounts, no analytics, no telemetry, and no tracking. We don't receive your chats, your name, your settings, or your files.

## Everything stays on your PC

- Chats and conversation history are saved locally in `Documents/eAI/conversations.json`.
- Settings such as your display name, selected model, and custom instructions are saved in `Documents/eAI/settings.json`.
- Your profile picture is saved in `Documents/eAI/avatars/`.
- Exported app icons are saved in `Documents/eAI/AppIcon/`.

Every file the app creates lives on your computer, and you can open, back up, or delete it at any time.

## The only network connections

eAI only connects to services you set up yourself:

- **Ollama**, running on your own machine or a server you control. Models run locally, so your messages never leave your computer.
- **SearXNG**, if you use your own instance. Otherwise, eAI falls back to DuckDuckGo for web search when a question needs it.
- **Model providers**, only if you add one yourself in a future version.

No other servers receive anything from eAI.

## You are in control

- Delete any chat from the sidebar, or delete the `Documents/eAI` folder to remove all data.
- Turn off web search in Settings to stop all outside lookups.
- Read, edit, and modify the source code yourself.
